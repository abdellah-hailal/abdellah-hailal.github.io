Cardiac arrhythmias on the MIT-BIH database

Recognise heartbeat types automatically, then measure what a premature beat changes in the beats that follow.

Context
Individual project, UQTR
Supervisor
Prof. Usef Faghihi
Data
MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database
Status
Methodology fixes in progress
  1. Question

    Can heartbeats be classified reliably, and does a premature beat really change the RR intervals that follow?

  2. Intervention

    Classification with MiniRocket, XGBoost and a 1D CNN + LSTM network. Rhythm modelling, then causal effect estimation with Double ML and CausalForestDML.

  3. Effect

    XGBoost reaches 0.74 accuracy. The average treatment effect (ATE) of a premature beat on the following RR intervals is significant.

These results are preliminary. My supervisor flagged nine issues, including data leakage between patients and post-treatment variables used as confounders. I am redoing the analysis with those fixed.

  • 1D CNN + LSTM

Renovision AI

Customers photograph their room, pick a product from the catalogue and see the result before buying.

Context
Personal project
My role
Design and full-stack development
Model
B2B2C SaaS for retailers
Status
Working MVP, pilot to validate
  1. Question

    In a renovation store, customers struggle to picture a floor or wall finish in their own room. Can we show it to them before they buy?

  2. Intervention

    A full-stack app: React and Vite on the client, FastAPI and SQLAlchemy on the server, JWT authentication, a catalogue with two photos per product, and AI image generation with a fallback provider.

  3. Effect

    A working MVP, ready for mobile with Capacitor. Next step: a pilot with a local retailer.
